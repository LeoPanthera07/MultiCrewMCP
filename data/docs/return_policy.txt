NovaMart Return Policy

NovaMart offers a standard 30-day return window for most items from the date of delivery. Items must be returned in their original packaging, unused, and with all original tags attached.

To initiate a return, customers must log in to their NovaMart account, go to order history, and print a pre-paid shipping label. A return shipping fee of $5.99 will be deducted from the final refund amount, except in cases where the return is due to a defective or incorrect item.

Special exceptions apply: electronics must be returned within 14 days and are subject to a 15% restocking fee if the box has been opened. Clothing and footwear can be returned up to 45 days, with no restocking fees, provided they are unworn and unwashed. Final sale items, marked clearly on the product page, are strictly non-refundable and cannot be exchanged. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at our central warehouse in Columbus, Ohio.
