NovaMart Apparel - Handling & Storage Guidelines

NovaMart provides premium clothing items, requiring careful climate-controlled storage to maintain fabric integrity.

Storage Rules:
- All leather garments and formal wear (including suits, blazers, and dresses) must be stored on wooden hangers inside breathable cotton garment bags. They should be placed in Bay B of Zone 1, which maintains a steady humidity level between 40% and 50%.
- Sensitive fabrics, such as 100% cashmere, silk, and merino wool, must be stored in airtight cedar bins to prevent moth damage. They must not be hung, as this stretches the fabric and ruins the garment's shape.
- Organic cotton items are sensitive to bright lights, which can cause color fading. These items must be kept in opaque boxes until they are picked for shipping.

Packing Instructions:
- Every apparel item must be folded using standard NovaMart acrylic folding boards to ensure uniform dimensions.
- Use a single sheet of acid-free tissue paper for lining and place the folded item in a biodegradable polybag before boxing. Avoid tight compression.
