NovaMart Shipping Policy

NovaMart provides reliable shipping services across the United States. We process all orders within 1 to 2 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or public holidays.

We offer three shipping methods at checkout:
1. Standard Shipping: Delivered within 5 to 7 business days. It is free for orders over $50, otherwise flat rate of $4.99.
2. Expedited Shipping: Delivered within 2 to 3 business days. Flat rate of $12.99.
3. Overnight Shipping: Delivered next business day for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST. Flat rate of $24.99.

Please note that delivery delays can occasionally occur due to extreme weather conditions, carrier capacity limits, or federal holidays.

Standard shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, and US Territories may take 10 to 14 business days and incurs a $9.99 surcharge. Customers will receive a shipment confirmation email with a tracking number within 24 hours of their package leaving our fulfillment center. Overnight orders placed after 1:00 PM EST on Fridays will be shipped on the following Monday.
